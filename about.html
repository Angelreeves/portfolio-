The Journey: 12 Years on the Mat

Wrestling has been the defining constant in my life for over a decade. Starting at the age of 4, I have grown up in wrestling rooms, learning that success is earned through consistent, grueling work and technical discipline. With 253 career wins, my experience has taught me how to remain composed in the third period and how to outmaneuver opponents through superior mat awareness.

My style is built on being an observant and tactical competitor. I don't just rely on athleticism; I study movement and leverage to find the most efficient path to a takedown or a turn. I thrive in the scramble and take pride in my ability to stay one step ahead of the competition technically.

Off the mat, I apply this same focus to my studies. I am currently on an accelerated path to graduate in 2027, demonstrating the same drive in the classroom that I show in training. I am looking for a college program that demands excellence and offers a culture of hard work and technical growth.
