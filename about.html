The Story Behind the Mat

Discipline. Observation. Architecture.

My Wrestling Journey

I have dedicated the last 12 years of my life to the sport of wrestling. What began as a childhood hobby has transformed into a lifestyle built on discipline and tactical precision. On the mat, I am an observant competitor; I focus on studying my opponent's movements and waiting for the right moment to execute with technical accuracy.

With a lifetime record of 253-54, I have learned that the hardest work happens when no one is watching. Whether it is a 5:00 AM workout or an extra film session, I thrive on the grind required to stay at the top of my game.

The Accelerated Path

I am currently a sophomore, but I am not following the traditional four-year high school path. I have committed to an accelerated graduation in 2027. This choice reflects my desire to jumpstart my future and enter the world of Architecture sooner.

I see a strong connection between wrestling and architecture: both require a solid foundation, a strategic plan, and the ability to adapt when faced with structural challenges. I am looking for a college program that will push me to excel in the design studio just as much as on the wrestling mat.
